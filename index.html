Field guide

Setup, interpretation and calibration

Place and stabilize the watch

Wind to a known state. Use a quiet surface, bring the microphone close to the case and keep the watch still. A compatible USB contact pickup can improve pulse separation. Avoid touching the case during acquisition.

Read the trace

The horizontal axis is sample-derived time. The vertical axis is arrival offset from the nominal beat grid. A fast watch slopes toward earlier arrivals. Alternating lanes reveal asymmetry. Zoom changes the visible time span.

Check lift angle and mode

Enter the movement maker’s lift angle. Swiss lever amplitude is shown only when recurring acoustic signatures support identifying the first and third pulses. Use rate-only mode for co-axial, detent or other unusual escapements.

Privacy and backups

Audio stays on this device. The browser stores watch profiles and records locally. Private browsing and storage eviction can remove records. Export a JSON backup regularly. Photos are included. Raw audio is optional and exported separately.

Independent audio-clock calibration

Record at least 60 seconds of repeated edges from a calibrated pulse source or GPS-disciplined generator through the same input and configuration. Count audio frames between known reference edges. Enter the independent elapsed time and the nominal sample rate of those frames.

Positive ppm means the audio clock counted too many samples per reference second. Applying it shortens the inferred beat period and raises the reported rate.

Start a microphone session to identify an input for a saved calibration.
